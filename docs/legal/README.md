# Legal documents: DRAFTS

**These are drafts for review by a qualified lawyer. They are not legal advice and must not be
published as-is.** They describe what the code actually does (as of P1, 2026-10-17) so the review
starts from facts. Every `[DECISION: ...]` needs an answer from you; every `[CHECK: ...]` needs
confirming against a contract or the law that applies to you (for example India's Digital
Personal Data Protection Act 2023, the EU/UK GDPR, or others, depending on where your users are).

| File | What |
|---|---|
| `privacy-policy.md` | What we collect, why, who processes it, rights, retention |
| `terms.md` | Terms of service, including the upload rights confirmation |
| `retention-policy.md` | How long each kind of data is kept, and how deletion works |
| `subprocessors.md` | Third parties that process personal data, and the contract checklist |
